Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what Furday collects, how it is used and the choices you have. In this policy, "Furday", "we" and "us" mean the team that runs the Furday app.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password. When you create a pet profile, we collect the pet's name, species, age, personality traits, likes, and any photo you choose to upload. When you post a diary entry, we collect the photos or videos, text, and any mood or scene tags you select. We also collect the comments, replies and messages you write, your friend requests, follows, likes and blocks, and the reports you submit. For each comment we record the time it was posted and a country name taken from the region setting on your device.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate the app's core features: creating diary entries (including generating AI written text based on your pet's profile and the photo you provide), displaying your posts to other users if you choose to share them, enabling likes, comments, replies, follows, friend requests, diary sharing and messaging between pets, and calculating features like companion score and weekly recaps.

AI Generated Content

When you use the AI diary feature, content you provide is sent to third party AI services. Photos you choose are sent to Anthropic to describe what is in them. The pet's details, your mood and scene choices, and the description of the photo are sent to DeepSeek to write the diary text. These services process the content to return a result for your diary. Please do not include information you do not want sent to these providers. Text written by the AI is labelled in the app.

Sharing With Other Users

Diary entries you mark as shared (not private) are visible to other users of the app, along with your pet's name and avatar. Comments and replies are visible to everyone who can see the diary they were posted on. You can share a diary with a friend, who then receives a card in Messages that opens that diary. If the diary is later deleted or made private, the card shows that the diary is no longer available. Messages you send are visible only to you and the person you are chatting with.

Comment Time and Country

Each comment shows when it was posted and a country name. The country comes from the region setting on the device at the time of posting. We do not use GPS or your precise location for this.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, our backend provider, in the Sydney, Australia region. Photos and videos are stored in cloud storage and served via public URLs. Image and video files may remain in storage for a period after the entry that used them has been deleted.

Your Choices

You can mark diary entries as private so they are not shown to other users, and switch an entry between private and public at any time. You can delete your own diary entries and your own comments inside the app. Deleting a diary also removes its likes, comments and mentions. You can block other pets to prevent them from seeing your content or messaging you. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete Account, which permanently removes your pets and their diary entries.

Content Moderation

We use an automated filter to check text for objectionable content before it is posted. You can report diary entries, comments and pet profiles that violate our zero tolerance policy on objectionable content and abusive behavior, and you can block pets you do not want to hear from. Reports are reviewed and acted upon within 24 hours. We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules.

Service Providers

We use Supabase for our database, file storage and sign in, and Anthropic and DeepSeek for the AI diary feature as described above. We do not sell your personal information.
