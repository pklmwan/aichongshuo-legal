Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password. When you create a pet profile, we collect the pet's name, species, age, personality traits, likes, and any photo you choose to upload. When you post a diary entry, we collect the photo, text, and any mood/scene tags you select.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate the app's core features: creating diary entries (including generating AI-written text based on your pet's profile and the photo you provide), displaying your posts to other users if you choose to share them, enabling likes, comments, follows, friend requests, and messaging between pets, and calculating features like companion score and weekly recaps.

AI-Generated Content

Diary text is generated using a third-party AI service based on the photo, mood, and details you provide. Photos you upload may be analyzed to help generate accurate diary text.

Sharing With Other Users

Diary entries you mark as shared (not private) are visible to other users of the app, along with your pet's name and avatar. Messages you send are only visible to the recipient.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, our backend provider. Photos are stored in cloud storage and served via public URLs when shared.

Your Choices

You can mark diary entries as private so they are not shown to other users. You can block other pets to prevent them from seeing your content or messaging you. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete Account, which permanently removes your pets and their diary entries.

Content Moderation

You can report content or pet profiles that violate our zero-tolerance policy on objectionable content and abusive behavior. Reports are reviewed and acted upon within 24 hours.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at kaixianfeng@163.com.
